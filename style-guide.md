## Interface

- Use the navy background for strong brand moments and navigation.
- Use gold for emphasis and primary calls to action; keep body text high contrast.
- Use technology blue for links, labels and focus indicators.
- Keep layouts fluid, keyboard accessible and comfortable on narrow screens.
- Follow `prefers-reduced-motion` and use visible focus styles.

## Editorial rules

- Only publish confirmed programme, contact and company information.
- Do not add unapproved testimonials, dates, fees, partnerships, awards or results.
- Edit future announcements in `assets/data/announcements.json`.
- Keep parent/guardian consent and the privacy notice available alongside registration.
