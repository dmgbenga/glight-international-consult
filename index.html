Practical learning notes

Good technology habits start early.

Simple ways to think clearly, build carefully and learn responsibly.

01 · PLAN

Break ideas into steps

Decide what the project should do, then split the task into smaller actions before writing code.

02 · TEST

Debug one change at a time

Run the project, observe what happens and adjust one part at a time to understand the result.

03 · USE AI THOUGHTFULLY

Check before you trust

Verify AI suggestions, protect personal information and use your own judgement when building.
