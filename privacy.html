For parents and guardians

How registration information is used

Glight International Consult Limited collects the parent/guardian name, email and phone number, and the student’s name, age, class, programme interest and preferred learning option when you submit a registration enquiry. You may also provide optional additional information.

The information is used to handle the enquiry and contact the parent or guardian about it. A parent or legal guardian must submit the form and provide consent. When email notifications are configured, Glight sends a confirmation to the parent/guardian email address and a minimal new-registration alert to Glight. Do not include medical, financial or identity information.

Storage and access

Registration information is sent to Glight’s server-side registration service and stored in a Supabase-hosted database. When Google Sheets is configured, a copy is also added to Glight’s designated spreadsheet for registration administration. Access to that copy is governed by the spreadsheet’s Google sharing settings. The site does not expose registration records to public visitors; the admin interface requires an authenticated administrator account. The selected Supabase data-hosting region depends on Glight’s project configuration.

The registration service also uses a keyed digest of the request IP address to limit automated submissions; the raw IP address is not included in the registration record. Expired rate-limit digests are removed during registration requests and by a scheduled cleanup task.

Retention and requests

Glight has not supplied a retention period for registration records. Please contact glightinternationalconsult@gmail.com to ask about access, correction or deletion of a registration, or to raise a privacy question.

Before accepting live registrations: Glight should approve a retention schedule, review this notice against its applicable privacy obligations and the Supabase project’s hosting region, and restrict Google Sheet access to staff who need it.

This notice describes the registration feature and is not legal advice.
