For parents and guardians

How registration information is used

Glight International Consult Limited collects the parent/guardian name, email and phone number, and the student’s name, age, class, programme interest and preferred learning option when you submit a registration enquiry.

The information is used to handle the enquiry and contact the parent or guardian about it. A parent or legal guardian must submit the form and provide consent. Do not include medical, financial or identity information.

Storage and access

Registration information is sent to Glight’s server-side registration service and stored in a Supabase-hosted database. The site does not expose registration records to public visitors; the admin interface requires an authenticated administrator account. The selected Supabase data-hosting region depends on Glight’s project configuration.

The registration service also uses a keyed digest of the request IP address to limit automated submissions; the raw IP address is not included in the registration record. Expired rate-limit digests are removed during registration requests and by a scheduled cleanup task.

Retention and requests

Glight has not supplied a retention period for registration records. Please contact glightinternationalconsult@gmail.com to ask about access, correction or deletion of a registration, or to raise a privacy question.

Before accepting live registrations: Glight should approve a retention schedule and review this notice against its applicable privacy obligations and the Supabase project’s hosting region.

This notice describes the registration feature and is not legal advice.
